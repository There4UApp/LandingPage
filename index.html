How It Works

Three steps. That's it.

When you need help, every second matters. No passwords. No menus. Just press the button.

01

Register

Create your profile, or set one up for a loved one. Add medical details and emergency contacts. Share a download link for instant access.

Every feature exists to remove friction between you and the help you need.

WhatsApp Alerts

Personalised emergency messages sent instantly via WhatsApp, the platform your contacts already use.

Your vital info, ready when you can't speak.

FAQ

Got questions?

Everything you need to know about There4U.

How does the WhatsApp alert work?

When you tap the button, There4U instantly sends a personalised WhatsApp message to every emergency contact on your list. The message includes your name, medical details, and a GPS link showing your location.

Can I set it up for my parents?

Absolutely. As an admin user, you can register a profile for anyone: a parent, a child, or a partner. Send them a download link or QR code. They'll open the app with everything pre-loaded and ready to go. No login needed.

What happens if I run out of alerts?

Top-up bundles are available for both monthly and annual billing, or take advantage of an early renewal. Your profile and contacts remain active regardless.

Does the app need to be open?

No. The recommended way to use There4U is via the home screen widget. One tap from your home screen triggers the alert, with no need to open the app first. Available on iOS and Android.

Is my personal data secure?

Your data is encrypted and stored securely. We never share your information with third parties. Your details are only transmitted to your chosen emergency contacts when you trigger an alert.
