Our Story

There4U began with a simple observation: the people who need personal safety technology the most (elderly parents, young dependents, vulnerable family members) are often the least equipped to set it up for themselves. Every existing solution asked the wrong person to do the hard work.

We built There4U to flip that equation. The person who cares does the setup. The person who needs protection just presses a button. One tap. Instant help. That's it.

Founded in Cape Town, South Africa, There4U is a personal-safety app that delivers one-tap emergency alerts over WhatsApp, the messaging platform billions of people already use. When triggered, the app sends a personalised message containing the member's name, medical details, and live GPS location to every emergency contact on file. No fumbling. No logins. No delays.

What Makes Us Different

Most safety apps require every user to create an account, remember a password, and manage their own setup. That's fine if you're tech-savvy. It's a non-starter if you're a 78-year-old grandparent who struggles with smartphones.

There4U uses a delegated registration model. A Main Member (typically an adult child, a partner, or a parent) handles all the setup, billing, and configuration. The person they're protecting receives a WhatsApp link, taps it, and is instantly connected. No registration. No passwords. No payment details. Just safety, ready to go.

We also made a deliberate choice to deliver alerts through WhatsApp rather than building a proprietary messaging system. Your emergency contacts already check WhatsApp, and an alert sent on a channel people actually use is an alert that actually works.

Our Mission

To become the default personal-safety layer for families and close-knit groups worldwide, making sure no one is ever truly unreachable when it matters.

Our Vision

A world where every person has a simple, reliable way to summon help and let the people who care about them know where they are, regardless of technical ability, location, or income level.

Our Values

Warmth Over Surveillance

There4U is about care and connection, not monitoring and control. Our brand, copy, and interactions reflect this distinction.

Safety technology should be accessible across markets, income levels, and payment infrastructures. We're building for everyone.

The same anxieties that drive our product here (a parent living alone, a teenager walking home, a commuter on a long drive) are felt by families everywhere.

Cape Town Startup There4U Reimagines Personal Safety With One-Tap WhatsApp Emergency Alerts

19 April 2026  ·  Cape Town, South Africa

Cape Town, South Africa, 19 April 2026: There4U, a new personal-safety app built in South Africa, today announced its founding and upcoming public launch, scheduled for Q4 2026. The app delivers one-tap emergency alerts over WhatsApp, sending a personalised message with the user's name, medical details, and live GPS location to their chosen emergency contacts, instantly.

What sets There4U apart from existing safety apps is its delegated registration model: a primary user (the "Main Member") handles all setup, billing, and configuration, while the person they're protecting (an elderly parent, a young child, a partner) simply taps a link, installs the app, and is immediately ready to call for help. No account creation. No passwords. No friction.

"We asked ourselves a simple question: why do safety apps make the most vulnerable person do the hardest work? There4U flips that. The person who cares does the setup. The person who needs protection just presses a button."

The app uses WhatsApp as its sole alert channel, a deliberate choice in markets like South Africa, where WhatsApp is the dominant messaging platform. Alerts are sent automatically through the WhatsApp Cloud API, meaning there are no per-message carrier fees and no proprietary messaging layer to learn.

There4U launches with two subscription plans tailored to the South African market: a Monthly plan at R40 and an Annual plan at R100 covering two members and up to four emergency contacts each. The Annual plan is positioned as the default for households (a parent and adult child, or a couple) at roughly R8 per month.

The company is headquartered in Cape Town, with expansion into Ireland, the UAE, Kenya, and India planned for 2027. The app is built with privacy at its core, complying with South Africa's POPIA and the EU's GDPR from day one.

There4U enters closed beta in Q3 2026 with 50–100 accounts in the South African market, followed by a public launch on the App Store and Google Play in Q4 2026.
