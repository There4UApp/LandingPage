FAQ

Got questions?

Everything you need to know about There4U.

How does the WhatsApp alert work?+

When you tap the Help Me button, There4U instantly sends a pre-written, personalised WhatsApp message to every emergency contact on your list. The message includes your name, medical details, and a live GPS link showing your exact location.

Can I set it up for my parents?+

Absolutely. As an admin user, you can register a profile for anyone — a parent, a child, or a partner. Once set up, send them a download link or QR code. They'll open the app with everything pre-loaded and ready to go. No login needed on their end.

What happens if I run out of alerts?+

You'll receive a notification when you're running low. You can top up with a single alert for R20, upgrade to a monthly plan, or switch to annual for the best value. Your profile and contacts remain active regardless.

Does the app need to be open to work?+

No. The recommended way to use There4U is via the home screen widget. One tap from your home screen triggers the alert instantly — no need to open the app first. Available on both iOS and Android.

Is my personal data secure?+

Your data is encrypted and stored securely. We never share your information with third parties. Your medical and personal details are only transmitted to your chosen emergency contacts when you trigger an alert.
