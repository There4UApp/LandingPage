Privacy Policy

Effective date: 19 April 2026  ·  Last updated: 19 April 2026

1. Introduction

There4U ("we", "us", "our") is a mobile emergency-alert application that delivers one-tap WhatsApp alerts containing a member's name, medical details, and GPS location to their chosen emergency contacts. We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, who we share it with, and what rights you have in relation to it. It applies to all users of the There4U mobile application and the there4u.app website.

We process personal information in accordance with the Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA) for South African users and the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) for users in the European Union and European Economic Area. Where we expand into additional markets, we comply with applicable local data protection legislation, including the UAE's PDPL, Kenya's DPA 2019, and India's DPDP Act.

Our guiding principle: We collect the minimum data necessary for the app to function. Your personal information exists to help you get help, nothing more.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information you provide directly

When you register and use There4U, we collect the following categories of personal information:

Data CategoryExamplesPurpose
Account credentials Email address, social login tokens (Google, Apple) Account creation, authentication, and account recovery
Member profile Full name, date of birth, city Personalising the emergency alert message sent to your contacts
Medical information Medical aid provider, member number, medical conditions Including relevant medical context in the alert message so responders can act appropriately
Emergency contacts Contact name, mobile phone number Delivering the WhatsApp alert to the people you have chosen
Payment information Subscription plan selection, transaction identifiers Managing your subscription. We do not store card numbers; payments are processed by Apple App Store, Google Play, or our payment processors (RevenueCat, Stripe)

2.2 Information about Members added via Shared Setup

If you are a Main Member and you add a second Member (for example, a parent, child, or partner), you provide their profile and emergency contact information on their behalf. By doing so, you confirm that you have obtained the necessary consent from that person, or that you have legal authority to provide their information (for example, as a parent or legal guardian).

The added Member does not create an account. Their device is authenticated using a device-bound token stored securely in the platform's native keychain (iOS Keychain or Android Keystore). We do not collect passwords or additional credentials from added Members.

2.3 Information collected automatically

  • Location data: When you trigger an alert, we access your device's GPS coordinates to include a location link in the WhatsApp message sent to your emergency contacts. We also store a last-known high-accuracy fix as a fallback. Location data is accessed only at the moment of an alert; we do not track your location continuously.
  • Device information: Device type, operating system version, app version, and a unique device identifier for authentication purposes.
  • Usage data: App interaction events (such as alert triggers, widget installs, and subscription events) used to improve the product and measure performance. This data is aggregated and does not identify you personally.

2.4 Information we do not collect

We do not collect call logs, SMS content, browsing history, contacts beyond those you explicitly add as emergency contacts, photos, or any content from your WhatsApp conversations. We do not engage in continuous location tracking or background surveillance of any kind.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your personal information for the following purposes:

  • Delivering emergency alerts: Composing and sending personalised WhatsApp messages containing your name, medical details, and GPS location to your emergency contacts when you trigger the alert.
  • Account management: Creating and maintaining your account, authenticating your identity, and managing your subscription and alert quota.
  • Shared Setup: Enabling the Main Member to configure and manage a second Member's profile and device access.
  • Product improvement: Analysing aggregated, anonymised usage patterns to improve app performance, reliability, and user experience.
  • Support: Responding to your enquiries and providing customer support.
  • Legal compliance: Meeting our obligations under applicable data protection laws and responding to lawful requests from authorities.

We will never sell your personal information, use it for advertising or profiling, or share it with third parties for their marketing purposes.

4. Legal Basis for Processing

We process your personal information under the following legal bases:

  • Consent: You provide explicit consent when you create your account and enter your personal, medical, and emergency contact details. For POPIA, this consent is genuine, voluntary, and specific to the stated purposes.
  • Performance of a contract: Processing is necessary to deliver the service you have subscribed to, specifically, sending emergency alerts to your chosen contacts.
  • Legitimate interest: We may process limited data for product improvement and security purposes, where our legitimate interest does not override your fundamental rights.
  • Legal obligation: We may process data where required by law, such as in response to a court order or regulatory requirement.

5. How We Share Your Information

Your personal information is shared only in the following limited circumstances:

  • With your emergency contacts: When you trigger an alert, the WhatsApp message containing your name, medical details, and GPS location is delivered to the emergency contacts you have designated. This is the core function of the service.
  • With service providers: We use third-party services to operate There4U, including cloud hosting providers, subscription management (RevenueCat), payment processing (Apple App Store, Google Play, Stripe), deep linking services, and analytics tools. These providers process data on our behalf under strict data processing agreements and are prohibited from using your data for their own purposes.
  • Where required by law: We may disclose information in response to valid legal requests from law enforcement or regulatory authorities, subject to our assessment of the request's legitimacy.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third party.

6. Data Security

We take the security of your personal information seriously and implement appropriate technical and organisational measures, including:

  • All API communication is encrypted using TLS 1.3.
  • Device-bound authentication tokens are stored in platform-native secure storage (iOS Keychain, Android Keystore).
  • Shared-setup tokens are cryptographically signed, time-limited, and single-use.
  • No sensitive personal data is stored in local logs or unencrypted storage.
  • We follow the OWASP Mobile Top 10 security guidelines.

While we implement commercially reasonable security measures, no method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure. We encourage you to protect your device with a screen lock and keep your operating system up to date.

7. Data Retention

We retain your personal information only for as long as necessary to provide the service and fulfil the purposes described in this policy. Specifically:

  • Active accounts: Your data is retained for the duration of your active subscription.
  • Lapsed accounts: If your subscription lapses, we retain your data for a reasonable grace period (typically 90 days) to allow for renewal, after which it is scheduled for deletion.
  • Deleted accounts: When the Main Member deletes their account, all associated data, including any added Member's data, is permanently deleted within 30 days.
  • Alert history: Records of triggered alerts are retained for a limited period for support and safety purposes and are deleted in accordance with our retention schedule.
